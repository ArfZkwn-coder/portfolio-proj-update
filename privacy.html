Privacy

Short version: this site sets no cookies and runs no third-party analytics. The only personal information it sends anywhere is what you type into the contact form, along with a little context — one thing at a time below.

What the contact form records

When you submit the form, these values are sent to my automation platform (Pabbly Connect) so I can reply:

  • your name, email address, chosen topic and message;
  • the page you submitted from and, if your browser sent one, the referring page;
  • any utm_ campaign parameters in the address you arrived on;
  • which of my outbound links you clicked on this visit — GitHub, LinkedIn or a certificate — and how many times;
  • basic browser context: your language, time zone, screen and window size;
  • a random identifier, regenerated every browser session, so those clicks can be matched to your submission. It is not tied to you and does not persist after you close the tab.

What it does not record

  • No cookies, and no third-party analytics, advertising or session-recording scripts.
  • No IP address is stored in your browser or sent on to me by this site.
  • No tracking at all on any page other than this portfolio and the résumé.

Why outbound clicks are counted

Knowing that a visitor looked at my GitHub before getting in touch tells me which part of the page did its job. Those clicks are counted in your own browser and are only attached to you if you go on to submit the form — a click on its own never leaves your browser and never reaches my automation.

Where it goes

Submissions are received by Pabbly Connect, which passes them to the tools my automation is built from — the ones I use to keep track of enquiries and reply to them. What those tools do with the data is my responsibility, and I keep it to replying to you and recording that we spoke. Ask me and I will delete anything I hold about you.

How to opt out

Send the Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal from a browser that supports it and this site stops sending anything at all: no click counts, and the form falls back to opening your own email app, which never touches my automation. Ad and script blockers will also stop it, and nothing on the page breaks if they do.
